## Verified High-Priority Additions

Each item below survived adversarial verification (reviews real, fitment checked, no recalls, sold in 2026). Copy caveats are mandatory edits before publishing.

### 1. Tesla Wall Connector (Gen 3, 48A NACS) — Tesla
- **Fits:** All models. Max rate depends on **battery, not drivetrain** — standard-range trims cap at 32A; Long Range/Performance (incl. Model 3 LR RWD) use full 48A.
- **Price:** $420–500 | **Amazon:** B0BWSK35WH
- **Why:** Default TMC/Reddit answer for Tesla-only households — cheapest 48A/11.5kW hardwired charger from a major brand, 24-ft cable, app scheduling, EnergySage "Old Reliable" (91/100).
- **Copy caveats:** Soften "near-bulletproof" (active law-firm investigation into overheating reports, usually installer lug torque); add "have a licensed electrician torque terminals to spec."
- **Sources:** energysage.com Gen 3 review; watsonschargingstations.com 2026 comparison; TMC thread 310182.

### 2. Tesla Mobile Connector (Gen 3) — Tesla
- **Fits:** All models/years (NACS). **Gen 2 discontinued April 2025** — recommend the current Gen 3 ($300, shop.tesla.com): same 20-ft cable, NEMA 5-15 + 14-50 adapters, 32A/~30 mi-hr, plus Cybertruck Powershare (V2L). Amazon "Gen 2" listings = leftover third-party stock.
- **Price:** $300 (Tesla direct)
- **Why:** Road-trip insurance; not included with new cars since April 2022, so a genuine first purchase for Highland/Juniper buyers.
- **Sources:** shop.tesla.com/product/mobile-connector; TMC threads 300821, 264270; notateslaapp.com/news/2636.

### 3. BASENOR Charging Cable Holder & Organizer — BASENOR
