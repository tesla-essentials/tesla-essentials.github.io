Tesla Model 3 Essentials

The Highland refresh Model 3 (and previous generations) benefit greatly from a few targeted accessories. Here are the ones that have proven their worth over years of ownership.

Protection First

  • Floor Mats — All-weather mats with good coverage are non-negotiable in many climates. Look for laser-measured fit.
  • Screen Protector — The new Highland display is gorgeous but a fingerprint and glare magnet. Matte versions are popular for daytime driving.
  • Door Liners / Puddle Lights Protection — Many owners add aftermarket door sill protectors because the factory ones scuff easily.

Organization

  • Center Console Organizer — Transforms the deep factory bin into usable space.
  • Rear Trunk Mat + Side Wall Protection — Especially useful if you regularly carry dirty items or pets.

Tech & Convenience

  • USB-C Hub — The Highland has updated ports; a small hub can expand options for charging and data.
  • License Plate Frame / Holder — Clean, no-drill options that look OEM and hold up in all weather.
