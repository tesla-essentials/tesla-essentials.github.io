Tesla Model Y Essentials Guide

The Model Y is an incredible vehicle, but a few well-chosen accessories can make ownership even better. Here are the items I consider essential after owning multiple Teslas (including a Model Y).

Interior Protection

Model Y owners put a lot of miles on their cars. Protecting the interior pays off quickly.

  • All-Weather Floor Mats — 3D MAXpider or WeatherTech style. Look for ones with tall walls and perfect edge fitment. The stock mats are too low and thin for real-world use.
  • Custom Sunshade — A good one (Covercraft or similar) makes a huge difference in cabin temperature on hot days and protects the dash and steering wheel from UV damage.
  • Screen Protector — Whether you prefer matte/anti-glare or glossy, get one with a good installation frame. Alignment on the large display is critical.

Storage & Organization

The Model Y has decent space, but it can be chaotic without the right organizers.

  • Frunk & Trunk Organizers — Collapsible or rigid bins that fit the exact dimensions of the frunk and rear trunk. Huge quality-of-life improvement for groceries, tools, and weekend gear.
  • Center Console & Armrest Tray — The factory console is deep and disorganized. A simple tray or divider set keeps small items from disappearing.

Tech & Everyday Carry

  • Right-angle USB-C cables — The ports are recessed. Straight cables get stressed and look messy.
  • USB-C Hub / Extension — Useful if you want to charge multiple devices or run a dashcam/Sentry SSD without using the main ports.
  • Jack Pads / Lift Pucks — Essential if you ever take the car in for service or want to do your own tire rotations. The factory jack points are easy to damage.

Pro Tip

Buy once, cry once. Cheap Amazon no-name accessories often fit poorly or wear out fast on a Model Y. The brands that have proven themselves across multiple Tesla generations (3D MAXpider, Covercraft, Spigen, etc.) are usually worth the small premium.

Model Y Specific Notes (2026+ Juniper)

The refreshed "Juniper" Model Y has a larger 16" display on higher trims and slightly different interior dimensions. Make sure any screen protector, sunshade, or mat you buy is explicitly listed for the new model year.
